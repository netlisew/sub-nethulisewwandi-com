§2The package rate

Committing to 6 sessions is worth something, so I am asking for a volume rate the way any block booking earns one: $25 off each session, or 7%. That takes each session from $350 to $325.

Commitment Off each Rate each Total

The ladder is there to show the shape, not to push you up it. If 6 goes well, the next tier is a conversation worth having.

The deposit is not discounted

The bond stays at your full $50 per session. The discount comes out of the balance paid on the day, never out of the money you hold. You still receive the same $300 up front you would have collected as deposits anyway — just sooner, and all at once.

What the discount actually costs you

Walk-up value 6 sessions at $350, if I booked them one at a time $2,100
Package discount $25 off each, for committing to all 6 −$150
Completion gratuity Paid on the final session if all 6 run to schedule +$100

Stated plainly so there is nothing buried: the discount is real and unconditional — you give up $150 the moment you agree. The gratuity is a separate, voluntary payment from me at the end, and it is how I intend to give most of it back for a term that runs cleanly. If the term does not complete, you keep the deposits and I do not pay the gratuity.

§3The formula

Package rate Rc = R − x  =  $350 − $25 $325
Bond B = n × d  =  6 × $50 $300
Paid on the day p = Rc − d  =  $325 − $50 $275
Package total T = B + (n × p)  =  n × Rc $1,950

The bond is not an extra payment on top. It is the same 6 deposits you would collect one at a time anyway — collected once, at the start, then drawn down by one deposit per session until it reaches zero.

§5Why the bond, and not prepayment

The common arrangement is to collect the next session's full fee at the current one. It secures continuity, and I understand why it exists — but it means one bout of flu costs me a whole session fee. The bond secures the same continuity with a much smaller hostage.

§7What happens when a date moves

Under 24 h Deposit kept, and the session counts as used. You held the slot; that is what the deposit is for. $50 + the slot

One rule underneath all five rows: a missed session can only ever cost me that session's deposit. Nothing reaches back into the other five.

§8Ending it early

  • Either of us can end the arrangement after any completed session. No explanation owed.
  • If you end it — the unused deposits still sitting in the bond come back to me, and the sessions already taken stay at the package rate.
  • If I end it — you keep one deposit as an exit fee, the rest of the bond comes back, and I pay the walk-up difference on the sessions I did take. Ending early means I did not earn the volume rate.
If you would rather not refund at all

Then make the bond non-refundable but non-expiring: whatever is left of it stays as credit against future sessions with you for 12 months. You keep the cash either way, and I keep a reason to come back rather than a grievance. I can live with this version.

§9What you get out of it

  • $300 in hand on day one, before a single session has happened — earlier and more certain than collecting 6 deposits one at a time, and not reduced by the discount.
  • 6 dates in your diary, agreed in one conversation instead of 6 separate booking negotiations.
  • No deposit chasing and no payment reminders for the whole term. The bond is already there.
  • $1,950 of committed revenue against the honest alternative, which is not $2,100 — it is however many ad-hoc bookings actually recur.
  • A completion gratuity of $100 on the final session if all 6 run to schedule, taking the term to $2,050 and the discount down to $50.
